================================================================================
NOTES
================================================================================

Every current image model garbles small text. Expect the shapes and the layout
to come out well and the labels to come out as convincing-looking nonsense.

Practical options, in order of how much text survives:
  1. gpt-image-1, Imagen 3, or Ideogram  - short labels usually legible
  2. Flux.1 dev                          - short labels sometimes legible
  3. SDXL / SDXL-Turbo / SD 1.5          - labels are decorative only

If the diagram is going in front of a reviewer, use
scripts/render_flow_diagram.py instead - it draws the same flow as SVG with
real, correct text.
